Safety Tips

Follow these simple safety tips to protect yourself and your information while using the internet.

Use Strong Passwords

Use at least 12 characters with letters, numbers, and symbols. Avoid using personal information.

Enable Two-Factor Authentication

Turn on 2FA whenever possible. It adds an extra layer of security to your accounts.

Practice Secure Browsing

Avoid suspicious links and only visit trusted websites that use https:// in the URL.

Keep Software Updated

Keep your operating system, apps, and antivirus updated to protect against threats.

Stay Safe Online!

Small steps today can prevent big problems tomorrow.
