Quick Cyber Facts

95% of cybersecurity breaches are caused by human error
Simple mistakes like weak passwords or clicking suspicious links can lead to attacks.
Cybercrime costs the world trillions every year
Businesses and individuals lose money due to scams, ransomware, and data breaches.
Phishing is the #1 cyber attack method
Fake emails and messages trick users into giving away passwords or personal data.
Strong passwords reduce hacking risks
Use long passwords with numbers, symbols, and uppercase letters.
Public Wi-Fi can be dangerous
Hackers may steal information on unsecured networks.
Software updates improve security
Updates fix vulnerabilities that hackers try to exploit.

Common Cyber Attacks

Phishing
Fake emails or messages designed to steal passwords or personal information.
Malware
Harmful software that can damage devices or steal data.
Ransomware
Hackers lock files or systems until money is paid.
Identity Theft
Criminals use stolen personal information for fraud.
Data Breaches
Unauthorized access to private company or customer information.
